Our Story

A Family-Run Gift Shop with a Personal Touch

Mimi & Papa's Gift Corner is a proud local gift shop right on Broadway in Albert Lea, Minnesota. We specialize in exclusive, customized items of your choosing — because a gift means more when it's made just for the person you're giving it to.

From custom apparel to hand-finished coasters and airbrushed fishing gear, everything we offer can be personalized. Stop in, tell us your idea, and we'll help bring it to life.
