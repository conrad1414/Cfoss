About the shop

Twenty-plus years behind the chair.

Got Time Barbershop is an old school barbershop in Ashland, Wisconsin, built on a simple idea: take the time to do a haircut right. With over 20 years of professional experience, every cut, fade, and shave gets the same attention to detail — no rushing, no shortcuts.

Stop in at 712 Main Street East for a classic barbershop experience, straight-razor sharp.
